    Guide

    How to use the studio

    Open any step for the details. Everything starts from the form at the bottom of this page.

    1. Send your brief

    Fill in the topic, length, shape and style, tick the scenes you want, and write the key points one per line. Press Copy brief and paste it into the Claude chat for this project. If you are unsure, leave the points empty and a hook, three points and a closing line will be proposed.

    2. Pick a style and a shape

    Try the styles in the live preview at the top of the page: the same video redraws in each one. Choose vertical 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, wide 16:9 for YouTube, or both. Vertical videos can be set to keep text clear of the app buttons.

    3. Approve the storyboard

    You get the scene list and the exact words first. Change anything you like; nothing is built until you say OK.

    4. Get your files

    A wide and a vertical MP4 with captions and sound effects but no music, a captions file and a cover image. Lay your own music over the MP4 in any editor or in Instagram. Every video is checked for text that runs off the frame or overlaps before it is sent. Watch it once with sound before you post.

    Add your logo and colours

    Send a logo file and, if you have them, your brand colours. If you only send the logo, the colours are picked from it. They apply to every scene and every style.

    Hindi videos

    Send the text in Hindi, or ask for a Hindi draft. On-screen text and captions are set in a Devanagari font. Have a Hindi reader check the wording before posting.

    Add a voiceover

    Each scene's caption line is what gets spoken. You can send your own recording of each line, or ask for an AI voice (English or Hindi). The scenes are then lengthened to fit the voice and the captions follow it. This step is still being tested, so ask before relying on it.

    Many videos from a spreadsheet

    For offers, units or personalised videos, send one row per video (name, price, code, phone and so on). One template then makes all of them in the same style.

    Real estate

    Choose Real estate in the form and add the project's RERA number. It is printed on every scene, and phrases that promise returns are rejected. Rules differ by state, so check yours before posting.

    FAQ

    Questions

    The short answers.

    Can I add my own music?

    Yes. The download has captions and sound effects only, so your track goes straight on top in any editor or in Instagram. Versions with a music bed can be made if you prefer.

    Which languages work?

    English and Hindi on-screen text and captions. A voiceover step (an AI voice, or your own recording, with the video timed to it) is built and still being tested, so ask before relying on it.

    Can it use my logo and colours?

    Yes. Send a name or logo and your colours, or let the studio pick colours from the logo. They apply to every style and every scene.

    What do I get back?

    A wide 16:9 and a vertical 9:16 MP4, a captions file, a cover image, and a short preview GIF if you want one for email.

    Are these videos AI-generated?

    The explainers are animated by code from your script, so the same brief always gives the same video. AI-generated clips are a separate kind of video, still in testing, and are always labelled as such.

    Do you handle real estate rules?

    The studio prints your RERA number on every scene and flags phrases such as assured returns. That helps, but the rules differ by state and the advertiser is responsible, so check yours before posting.

    How is a video checked?

    Each plan is validated, then every scene is tested in the chosen style, wide and vertical, for text that runs off the frame or overlaps. A person then looks at the result before it is sent.

    What does it cost?

    Pricing is being worked out while we test. Send a brief and you will get a quote before anything is built.
